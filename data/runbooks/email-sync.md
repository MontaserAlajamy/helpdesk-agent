# Email is not syncing or not sending
Arabic: الإيميل ما بيحدث، البريد ما بيرسل، الرسائل عالقة في صندوق الصادر، أوتلوك
1. Check if webmail works in a browser. If webmail also fails, the mail service may be down: check the "mail" service.
2. If webmail works, the problem is the app: close Outlook, reopen it, and check "Work Offline" is off.
3. Look in the Outbox: a message with a very large attachment (over 25 MB) blocks sending. Remove it or share a link.
4. Mailbox full warning: archive or delete old mail, empty Deleted Items.
Escalate: if the mail service is down, open a ticket (category email, priority high) - many people are affected.
