# Printer offline or not printing
Arabic: الطابعة ما بتطبع، الطابعة غير متصلة، أوفلاين، ورق عالق
1. Check the printer screen for paper jams, empty trays or low toner, and clear them.
2. Turn the printer off, wait 30 seconds, turn it on.
3. On your computer, open the print queue, cancel stuck jobs and print a test page.
4. Make sure you are on the office network, not guest Wi-Fi; guest Wi-Fi cannot see printers.
Escalate: hardware errors on the printer screen (fuser, drum, error codes) need a ticket (category hardware, priority low).
