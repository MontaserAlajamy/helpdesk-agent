# Wi-Fi slow or dropping
Arabic: الواي فاي بطيء، النت بطيء، الشبكة بتقطع، الإنترنت ضعيف
1. Run a speed test and note the result; compare with a colleague in the same room.
2. Forget the office Wi-Fi network and join again.
3. Move closer to an access point; meeting rooms with closed doors have weaker signal.
4. Use a cable at your desk for video calls when possible.
Escalate: if the whole floor is slow, open a ticket (category network, priority high) with the speed test results.
