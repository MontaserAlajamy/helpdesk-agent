# Forgot password or account locked
Arabic: نسيت كلمة السر، كلمة المرور، الحساب مقفول، الحساب مغلق، ما قادر أسجل دخول
1. Use the self-service reset page from the login screen ("Forgot password"); it sends a code to your phone.
2. A locked account unlocks by itself after 15 minutes. Do not keep trying: each failed try restarts the timer.
3. New passwords need 12 characters and must not repeat your last 5 passwords.
4. After a reset, update the password on your phone mail app too, or it will lock the account again.
Escalate: if you have no access to the registered phone, open a ticket (category access, priority medium).
Never send a password in chat or email; the helpdesk never asks for it.
