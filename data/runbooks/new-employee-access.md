# New employee: accounts and access
Arabic: موظف جديد، حساب جديد، صلاحيات، تفعيل الحساب، إيميل لموظف جديد
1. Access is requested by the manager, not by the new employee.
2. The request needs: full name, job title, start date, team, and the systems needed (email, VPN, shared drives).
3. Accounts are created within one working day of the request.
Escalate: always open a ticket for new access (category access, priority medium); access is never granted in chat.
