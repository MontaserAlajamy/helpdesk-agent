# VPN will not connect
Arabic: الـ VPN ما بيوصل، مشكلة اتصال الشبكة الافتراضية الخاصة، العمل من البيت
1. Confirm the internet works without the VPN (open any public website).
2. Check the time and date on the laptop are correct; a wrong clock breaks the VPN certificate check.
3. Quit the VPN client fully, start it again and sign in with your company email.
4. If it says "authentication failed", your password may have expired: follow the password reset runbook.
5. Try another network (phone hotspot). If it works there, the home router blocks the VPN port.
Escalate: if steps 1-5 fail, open a ticket (category network, priority medium; high if you cannot work at all).
